Analytics tier

Real-time detections and hunting. 90 days of retention are included in the ingestion price, and this is the part the commitment tier discounts. Copied to the data lake automatically.

Data lake

Lake-only tables and long-term retention of analytics data. Billed per GB ingested and stored, searched with KQL queries and jobs. No real-time detections.
